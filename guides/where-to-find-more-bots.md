## Notes

- These are third-party, unofficial bots. They are not made or endorsed by xAI. Read each page before adding.
- Almost all ask for approval before sending, filing, deleting, or blocking.
- Start with one or two and expand once you see how they behave.
- Original stack source: [unicode’s post](https://x.com/unicodef1wn/status/2115049336543326710)
- Most first-round finds came from [Grok Bot Shelf](https://grokbotshelf.com) (community directory, ~595 bots, curated by [@Phil\_Holland](https://x.com/Phil_Holland) and [@thecsguy](https://x.com/thecsguy) — browse it for more). A few (Pack Out, Pit Stop, Insurance Aware, Cue, Course Builder & Coach) were found directly in X posts.
- The October 27, 2026 security and home-network finds came from Grok Bot Shelf, [Grok Bot Index](https://grokbotindex.com), [grokbot.dev](https://grokbot.dev), [GrokBot101](https://grokbot101.org), [Hire a Grok Bot](https://hireagrokbot.com) and X posts (for example Tripwire, UniFi Network Admin, Tailscale Admin, Wifi Wendy, Inbox Guardian).

# General Best Practices

[← Back to README](../README.md)

General advice, not sourced, so use your judgment.

- Add one or two bots at a time, watch a few runs, then add more.
- Start every new bot read-only or draft-only; give it permission to act only after it has earned trust.
- Keep a short note per bot: its one job, its routines, and what it may never do.
- Recheck privacy removals monthly at first, then quarterly once listings stay gone.
- Space routines out (for example different hours or days) so they don't all fire at once.
- Pause bots you haven't used in a month rather than letting their routines run.
- Back up bots you've tuned heavily before experimenting with big changes.
- Never paste passwords or card numbers into chat; use the secure sign-in and vault flows.

These are third-party, unofficial bots. Review each one before you add it. Almost all ask for approval before sending, filing, deleting, or blocking.
